# Swim Celebrator

## What it does
- asks you your distance
- asks you your stroke and gives you tips based on the stroke
- asks your pb
- asks your time
- checks whether you broke your pb
- tells you comparing to your pb
- asks if you want to do it again
- if yes repeat
- if no tells you to race well

## How to run it(in terminal)
`cargo new swim_celobrator`
paste this into `main.rs`
`cd swim_celobrator`
`cargo run`

## What it prints
if you do a valid stroke it prints tips for that stroke
if you break your pb it congratulate you
if you say not to doing it again it says bye race well
